## 9. Limitations

- Synthetic world: metrics measure the pipeline against a simulator, not reality.
- The simulator and the engine share well-established physics (heat loss, degree days); hidden behaviour, installation quality and record errors keep evaluation honest, but some optimism remains.
- Value premiums are associational; the planted premiums come from the literature's order of magnitude.
- Small samples: some measures have few historical retrofits; rates are shrunk toward the engineering estimate.
- Held-out predictions: [`test_predictions.csv`](test_predictions.csv), schema in [`TEST_PREDICTIONS_SCHEMA.md`](TEST_PREDICTIONS_SCHEMA.md).
