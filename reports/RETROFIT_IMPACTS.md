# Retrofit impacts on the brief's own quantities (PS2 §4)

> Generated by `uv run greeniq evaluate`. Each measure done **alone** on today's home, over metered **validation** homes, with the product's savings model (calibrated: real-home evidence for loft insulation, cavity wall insulation, solar pv; simulated history for the rest). The brief's figures are illustrative and each row refers to its own quantity; per PS2 §4 our catalogue takes precedence, so this table documents how the two compare. Real-home figures are measured in DESNZ NEED (difference-in-differences) on a related but not identical quantity, shown in the last column.

Notes:

- Space-heating and heating-energy percentages use gas-heated metered homes, where the meter regression splits gas into heating and the rest.
- Loft, cavity and solar are corrected to what real UK homes achieved (median ÷ median realisation, reports/REAL_DATA.md); that is why solar sits well below the brief's 20-35% and close to the measured 9.75%.
- Heat pump: the brief's 15-25% is far below our engine. Replacing a gas boiler (≈85-90% efficient) with a heat pump (seasonal COP 2.6-3.4 in our catalogue; 2.8 is the Boiler Upgrade Scheme minimum) divides the energy bought for heat by roughly three, so purchased kWh typically fall by half even though electricity rises. We keep the physics and flag the gap; there is no real UK heat-pump retrofit evidence in NEED to calibrate against.
