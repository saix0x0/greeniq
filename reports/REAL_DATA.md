# Real-data evidence: DESNZ NEED, 50,000 real homes

> Generated by `uv run python -m greeniq real-evidence`. Source: DESNZ National Energy Efficiency Data-Framework, anonymised 2026, 50,000-row sample. Contains public sector information licensed under the Open Government Licence v3.0. File checksum verified: **True**. **These are real homes and real meter readings**, unlike the simulated portfolio used elsewhere in the app.

Homes: 50,000 (split train 35,197 · validation 7,311 · test 7,492). Valid 2024 readings: gas 37,186 (median 10,000 kWh), electricity 45,214 (median 2,600 kWh). Invalid, estimated or capped readings are treated as missing, never as zero.

## 1. Predicting a home's energy use from its characteristics (real held-out homes)

**Electricity**: 6,774 real test homes (trained on 31,837).

| Model | MAE kWh | RMSE kWh | R² | Median APE |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| National median (naive) | 1,683 | 2,744 | -0.069 | 44.4% |
| Median of similar homes (simple baseline) | 1,601 | 2,647 | 0.005 | 40.0% |
| Gradient boosting (GreenIQ) | 1,640 | 2,436 | 0.157 | 43.9% |

Gradient boosting **does not beat** the similar-homes baseline on real held-out homes (-2.4% lower MAE). Promoted on validation: **peer**. Median APE by type: Bungalow 44.8% · Detached 41.2% · End terrace 41.9% · Flat 48.5% · Mid terrace 42.3% · Semi detached 42.7%.

**Gas**: 5,600 real test homes (trained on 26,105).

| Model | MAE kWh | RMSE kWh | R² | Median APE |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| National median (naive) | 4,799 | 6,665 | -0.030 | 35.9% |
| Median of similar homes (simple baseline) | 3,979 | 5,525 | 0.292 | 29.9% |
| Gradient boosting (GreenIQ) | 3,857 | 5,283 | 0.353 | 28.5% |

Gradient boosting **beats** the similar-homes baseline on real held-out homes (3.1% lower MAE). Promoted on validation: **gbm**. Median APE by type: Bungalow 26.9% · Detached 23.4% · End terrace 27.6% · Flat 39.3% · Mid terrace 30.4% · Semi detached 27.3%.

*Interpretation.* Characteristics explain only part of real energy use; occupants and behaviour explain the rest, so errors stay large even for the best model. That is why GreenIQ anchors savings on each home's **own meter readings** and uses these models only as a labelled estimate when readings are missing, and as the 'homes like yours' benchmark.

## 2. What retrofits really saved (difference-in-differences on real installs)

For each home that installed a measure in year *y*: the change in use from *y−1* to *y+1*, minus the same change in similar homes (type, age band, floor-area band, region) that never installed loft, cavity or solar, reported as the **median** over installers (robust to the few homes with tiny "before" use) with a bootstrap 90% interval. Gas is weather-corrected by DESNZ; solar is measured on grid electricity.

| Measure | Fuel | Homes (train+val) | Real saving | 90% interval | ≈ kWh/yr | Test-split check | GreenIQ engine predicts | Realisation |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| loft | gas | 4,452 | **5.6%** | 5.2% to 6.1% | 829 | 7.3% (n=768) | 6.4% | 0.87 |
| cavity | gas | 3,725 | **11.1%** | 10.5% to 11.6% | 1,613 | 11.6% (n=644) | 22.1% | 0.50 |
| solar pv | electricity | 381 | **9.8%** | 5.2% to 12.2% | 310 | 7.3% (n=71) | 26.2% | 0.37 |

*Realisation* = real saving ÷ the engine's mean predicted saving for that measure across the app's homes (engineering estimate, uncalibrated). Below 1 means real installs save less than physics predicts. This is the well-known performance gap, which can come from top-up installs, comfort take-back or installation quality.

## 3. Limitations

- NEED has no wall type, loft depth, glazing, controls, boiler type, exact floor area or prices, so the app's detailed homes still come from the documented simulator. Every field is labelled.
- Install years are for the *latest* government-scheme install (it may be a top-up), and installers chose to install: the savings are observed associations with a matched comparison, not a randomised trial.
- Consumption is rounded to 100 kWh and capped (gas 1,000–50,000; electricity 500–25,000 kWh).
- Electricity is not weather-corrected; the matched comparison removes the shared year effect.
