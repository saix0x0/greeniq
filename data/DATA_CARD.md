# GreenIQ data card

> **Everything in `data/` is synthetic.** No real person, address, meter or sale is
> represented. It is generated by a seeded, documented simulator
> ([`greeniq/simulator/world.py`](../greeniq/simulator/world.py), version `greeniq-world/1.0`)
> and can be regenerated byte-for-byte with `uv run greeniq generate` (seed 42, 3,000 homes).

## 1. Why synthetic, and why a *simulator* rather than labels

PS2 permits synthetic data but warns that "arbitrary synthetic labels… may reduce your
score". So we don't assign labels. We simulate a world with plausible physics (heat loss,
weather, heating efficiency), people (occupancy, heating behaviour), installers
(installation quality) and a housing market, and then **observe it the way a lender
would**: property records with gaps and errors, monthly meter reads with gaps, and sale
prices. Every "label" used in evaluation is an observation of this world, not a rule we
wrote to match our own engines.

Public datasets considered (PS2 suggests them) and why they are not the primary source in v1:

| Dataset | Licence | Why not primary in v1 |
|---|---|---|
| EPC register (England & Wales) | OGL, but bulk download needs an account | Needs account creation; no meter data; does not join to smart-meter data at property level |
| London Smart Meter data | CC BY | No property characteristics; cannot be joined to EPCs at home level |
| NEED | OGL | Published tables are **aggregated**, not per home |
| HM Land Registry Price Paid | OGL | Joinable to EPCs by address only with fuzzy matching; no energy data |

Because these sources do not join at the individual-home level, a coherent per-home dataset
(characteristics + meters + sales + retrofits) has to be simulated. The data seam
(`greeniq/data.py`) is where real loaders would plug in later.

### Current-value vs historical-price separation (DR-4)
`sales.csv` holds **historical sale prices** with the EPC band *at sale*. There is no
"current value" column anywhere in `raw/`: the current value is an **output** of the value
model. The true 2026 value exists only in `truth/hidden.csv` for evaluation.

## 3. How the world is generated

**Homes.** Region (7), type (detached 17% · semi 25% · terraced 28% · bungalow 8% · flat 21%),
construction age (6 bands, flats skew newer), floor area (log-normal by type), walls
(solid mostly pre-1919; cavity fill ≈65% for older cavities, built-in for 1981+), loft depth,
floor insulation, glazing, heating (gas share by region; boiler age 0–30 years, condensing if
≤ 19 years; heat pumps more common off-gas), controls, solar PV (~6% of houses), roof
orientation/shading, listed/conservation status, outdoor space.

**Heat loss.** Geometry from floor area and type → wall/roof/floor/window areas; U-values by
construction and insulation; ventilation by age; +10% thermal bridging → heat loss
coefficient (W/K). Space heat = HLC × degree days × 24 / 1000, scaled by a global calibration
constant (0.86).

**People and behaviour (hidden).** Occupants (1–5), heating-behaviour multiplier
(log-normal, σ 0.18), appliance-use multiplier (σ 0.20), daytime occupancy (affects solar
self-consumption), gas hob (60% of gas homes).

**Realism the engines are *not* told about (hidden):**
- **Prebound effect:** leaky homes are under-heated in practice (multiplier falls from 1.0
  to 0.72 as heat loss per m² rises), so insulation saves less than physics predicts and
  some of the improvement is taken as comfort.
- **Installation quality:** wall insulation realises only 60–100% of its theoretical U-value
  improvement.
- **Heat-pump performance:** SCOP falls in leaky homes (3.35 → 2.45) plus installation noise (σ 0.25).
- **Smart thermostats:** 0–10% heating reduction, varying by household.
- **Boiler efficiency:** degrades with age, plus unit-to-unit noise.

**Meters.** Monthly purchased energy per fuel with 4% multiplicative read noise. Solar
generation (regional yield × orientation × shading × monthly profile) is self-consumed up to
35–50% (more if someone is home in the day), and only the remainder offsets imports.

**EPC assessor.** A SAP-2012-style rating: standard occupancy (SAP formula from floor area),
standard climate (2,100 degree days), SAP-2012-like prices (gas 3.48p, electricity 13.19p,
off-peak 5.5p), PV credited, ECF = 0.42 × cost / (TFA + 45), SAP = 117 − 121·log₁₀(ECF) if
ECF ≥ 3.5 else 100 − 13.95·ECF. Assessor noise σ 2.5 SAP points. This is **not** an
accredited SAP calculation.

**Market.** Value = regional £/m² × area^0.85 × type and age factors × hidden location
premium (log-normal σ 0.15) × (1 + EPC premium) × regional index × sale noise (σ 0.05).
**Planted EPC premiums vs band D:** A +5.0% · B +4.0% · C +1.8% · E −1.0% · F −2.5% · G −6.0%,
chosen to match the order of magnitude reported by Fuerst et al. (DECC 2013; Energy
Economics 2015). **The value model can only rediscover what was planted here.**

## 4. Recorded-data imperfections (deliberate, to test robustness)

| Imperfection | Rate | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Missing fields | 3–35% depending on field (see `validation_report.json`) | Exercises defaults, estimates and "partial" scores |
| No EPC on record | 11.8% | Rating must be estimated |
| **Stale EPC** (lodged before a recorded retrofit) | 487 homes | Score must not trust an out-of-date rating blindly |
| **Wrong heating record** (gas boiler recorded as heat pump) | 24 homes | Conflict detection (meters show gas heating) |
| Missing monthly reads | 4% of reads | Annualisation must not zero-fill |
| New customers (3–5 months of reads) | ~5% | "Insufficient" annualisation → model estimate |
| No meter data | ~2% | Baseline unavailable from meters |
| Void homes (all reads zero) | 27 | Baseline zero → improvement potential unavailable |

## 5. Splits (DR-3)

Splits are by **customer**, not by row or property: `sha256("greeniq-split-v1:" + customer_id)`
→ train 70% / validation 15% / test 15%. All of a customer's homes, and all their monthly
rows and sales, land in the same split. Counts: **train 2,146 · validation 453 · test 401** homes.
Tests assert no customer or property appears in two splits. The test split is used **once**,
for the final report; tuning uses validation only.

## 6. Annualisation (DR-5)

Degree-day regression per fuel (`kWh = a·days + b·HDD`, a, b ≥ 0), evaluated in a normal
weather year, over the latest 12 months. Status counts in this dataset:

| Fuel | metered_full | metered_partial | insufficient | zero | no_data | not_applicable |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Electricity | 1,682 | 1,093 | 125 | 27 | 73 | — |
| Gas | 1,334 | 868 | 98 | 62 | 65 | 573 |

"Zero" for gas includes heat-pump homes with a gas connection but no gas use (a real zero,
not a missing value). "Not applicable" means the home has no gas connection.

## 7. Calibration against public anchors

| Statistic | This dataset | Public anchor |
|---|---|---|
| Median annual gas, gas-heated homes | 12,120 kWh | Ofgem TDCV medium: 11,500 kWh |
| Median annual electricity (all metered homes) | 3,077 kWh | Ofgem TDCV medium: 2,700 kWh (dual fuel) |
| Recorded EPC bands | A 0.4% · B 1.7% · C 27.4% · D 41.6% · E 13.2% · F 2.8% · G 1.1% · none 11.8% | England's stock is somewhat better (more C); ours skews older, which gives the ranking task enough Poor homes |
| Property type mix | terraced 28% · semi 25% · flat 21% · detached 17% · bungalow 8% | Close to English Housing Survey proportions |

The all-homes electricity median is above the dual-fuel TDCV because ~19% of homes heat with electricity.

## 8. Limitations

- Synthetic. Agreement between our models and this world shows the **pipeline** works; it is
  **not** evidence of real-world accuracy.
- The physics is "lite" (steady-state degree days, no solar gains model, no dynamic effects).
- Weather, regional prices and the market index are synthetic, shaped like UK patterns
  but not official data.
- Planted value premiums come from the literature's order of magnitude; the recovered uplift
  inherits that assumption.
- Only electricity and gas (no oil, LPG or biomass), as PS2's carbon formula specifies.
